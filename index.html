I have a background in electrical engineering, and I wanted a career where creativity and analytics meet, especially around fabric and colour. I've always had a passion for style and fashion, and a dream of one day having my own clothing brand.

That dream started with Asoo Design, my handmade jewelry brand. When I moved to Canada, I joined lululemon and worked in visual merchandising.

On the floor, I look for gaps. One stood out: our store had a data dashboard, but it was rarely used and never really reached the team. So I took it on, translating the numbers into simple insights and stories, so we could all make better-informed decisions.

I also listen. In the fitting room, guests tell you exactly what they want and what's missing. I watch how displays perform and how head office decisions play out on our floor. The numbers tell me what happened; the store tells me why. Bringing the two together is how I learn what customers are really looking for, and it's the work I want to grow in: merchandising and planning.
