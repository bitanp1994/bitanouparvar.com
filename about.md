I was born and raised in Tehran, Iran, and have lived in Canada since 2023.

I hold a master's degree in electrical engineering, specializing in power electronics, from K. N. Toosi University of Technology. After that, I worked as a research and development engineer at Niroo Research Institute (NRI), and as a technical engineer at Petro Tajhiz Daria.

Alongside my engineering work, my lifelong love of art led me to start making handmade jewelry, especially earrings, and I built a small brand around it, [Asoo Design]({{ '/projects/asoo-design/' | relative_url }}). Little by little, I realized I couldn't picture my future as an engineer. I loved fabric, colour and, most of all, tangible results. I didn't want to work with high voltage and current, converters and inverters. So in 2022 I decided to leave engineering and follow my passion.

In 2023 I moved to Canada. I wanted to work in the apparel industry, and my first job here was as an educator at a lululemon store. Starting from scratch has not been easy, and it still isn't. A new country, a new language and a new culture bring a lot of cognitive load.

I've now been at lululemon for nearly two and a half years, as an educator and as the store's visual merchandiser. I'm working to find, or better said, *make* my path forward. I want to grow in retail, merchandising and planning.

Here, I share that journey, along with my thoughts and ideas about this industry.
