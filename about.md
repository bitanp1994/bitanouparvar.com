My name is Bita Nouparvar. I was born and raised in Tehran, Iran, and have lived in Canada since 2023.

I hold a master's degree in electrical engineering, specializing in power electronics, from K. N. Toosi University of Technology. Following my studies, I worked as a research and development engineer at the Niroo Research Institute (NRI) and as a technical engineer at Petro Tajhiz Daria. Alongside my engineering career, my lifelong love for art led me to start crafting handmade jewelry, eventually building a small brand around it called [Asoo Design](/projects/asoo-design/). Over time, I realized I couldn't picture my long-term future in a traditional engineering setting. I found myself drawn to fabric, color, and most of all, tangible real-world results. I no longer wanted to work with high voltage, currents, converters, and inverters. In 2022, I chose to leave the engineering field entirely to pursue this true passion.

In 2023, I moved to Canada with a clear goal: to build a career in the apparel industry, starting my journey here as an educator at a lululemon store. Starting from scratch in a new country, with a new language and culture, created a massive cognitive load, and it hasn't been easy.

I have now been with lululemon for nearly two and a half years, contributing both as an educator and as our store’s visual merchandiser. Today, I am focused on actively building and making my path forward as I look to grow within corporate retail, merchandising, and planning.

This portfolio is a space where I share that personal journey, along with the direct thoughts, observations, and ideas I have gained about this industry.
