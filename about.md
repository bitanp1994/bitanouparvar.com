I grew up surrounded by fabric. My mother is a seamstress, and as a child I loved sketching designs and watching her turn them into real clothes. That was my first lesson in what I still love most: an idea becoming something you can hold and wear.

I chose engineering anyway. I earned a master's degree in electrical engineering, specializing in power electronics, from K. N. Toosi University of Technology, then worked as a research and development engineer at the Niroo Research Institute (NRI) and as a technical engineer at Petro Tajhiz Daria. Engineering taught me how to break a problem down and build a system that solves it. But I kept coming back to fabric, colour and tangible results, not converters and inverters.

So I started teaching myself. I followed a designer's fashion design lessons online, learned colour theory and the basics of garment construction, and studied how inspiration becomes a story. I read about the anatomy of the body and how clothes move with it. I brought all of it into handmade jewellery, and built a small brand around it: [Asoo Design](/projects/asoo-design/). In 2022, I left engineering to follow this path fully.

In 2023, I moved to Canada with one goal: to build a career in the apparel industry. I started as an educator at a lululemon store, and later also became the store's visual merchandiser. Starting from scratch in a new country, with a new language and culture, has not been easy. But it gave me a new kind of education. Every day I hear guests' stories, see what they need and what they can't find, and watch how the market competes for them. Alongside the floor, I've been learning the business side: I completed Google's certificate in digital marketing and e-commerce, studied *Storytelling with Data* and used it to build our store's weekly insights report, and researched Alo Yoga to understand why our guests shop there too.

That is where I stand today: between engineering and art. I'm a problem solver who loves storytelling, and I want to bring both to merchandising and planning, where the numbers and the stories behind a product meet.

Here you'll find the projects behind that work, and a journal of what I learn on the floor.
