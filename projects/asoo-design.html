Projects / Jewelry making

Asoo Design

My handmade jewelry brand, founded in Tehran in 2020.

Each pair of earrings is designed and stitched by hand from coiled cord, beads and crystals. Running Asoo Design taught me the full life of a product, from design and production to marketing and sales, long before I worked in retail. It's also where my love of colour and texture turned into something people could wear.

Collection

Courage

This collection was made in memory of the young women and men of my homeland who lost their lives during the Woman, Life, Freedom movement. It was a dark time, and I worked only in black and red.

Collection

Spring / Summer

My spring and summer collection, from my last year in Iran. I challenged myself with a new material: sermeh, a fine coiled metal thread, stitched carefully by hand with invisible thread. It was harder to work with, and that was the point. The colours came straight from spring, and I photographed every piece on the rooftop of our home.
