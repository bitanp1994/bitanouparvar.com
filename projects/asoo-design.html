Projects / Jewelry making

Asoo Design

My handmade jewelry brand, founded in Tehran in 2020.

Each pair of earrings is designed and stitched by hand from coiled cord, beads and crystals. Running Asoo Design taught me the full life of a product, from design and production to marketing and sales, long before I worked in retail. It's also where my love of colour and texture turned into something people could wear.
