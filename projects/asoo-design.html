Asoo Design

My handmade jewelry brand, founded in Tehran in 2020.

Asoo Design began during the pandemic, around the time I decided to slowly step away from engineering. I wanted to work with my hands, with colour and material, and to see an idea turn into something real. Exploring colour pulled me in first, and before long I was releasing collections.

Then came the business side: figuring out where to sell, from Instagram to physical galleries, and learning who I was really designing for. My customers turned out to be people looking for distinctive pieces, things they wouldn't find anywhere else. Asoo taught me the whole life of a product, from a first sketch to the person who wears it.

Collection

Bravery

This collection was made in memory of the young women and men of my homeland who lost their lives during the Woman, Life, Freedom movement. It was a dark time, and I kept the palette to just two colours: black for the grief we carried, and red for their bravery.

Each piece is made from soutache, crystals and stones, all sewn together by hand. They are dense and heavy, with the crystals gathered close at the centre, like something held onto. Making them was my way of staying with that time, and of remembering.

Collection

Spring / Summer

My spring and summer collection, from my last year in Iran. I challenged myself with a new material: sermeh, a fine coiled metal thread, stitched carefully by hand with invisible thread. It was harder to work with, and that was the point. The colours came straight from spring, and I photographed every piece on the rooftop of our home.
