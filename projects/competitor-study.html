Competitor Study: Alo Yoga

From the store floor, I can see who walks in. I wanted to understand who else they shop with, and why.

01 / The market

LULULEMON

The reference point for premium technical apparel, built on decades of trust in athletic engineering and performance fabrics.

Top competitors

1

ALO YOGA

Streetwear-first

Activewear as a status symbol, fast influencer marketing, and a large overlap with our shoppers.

2

VUORI

Gym to office

Premium versatility, wholesale partners like Nordstrom, and a strong menswear heritage.

3

ATHLETA

Inclusive

A multi-generational brand with a wide size range and values-driven positioning.

Emerging competitors

1

ON

Footwear first

Extending its running authority into performance apparel.

2

OFFLINE BY AERIE

Accessible

A fast-growing active legging brand at a lower price point.

3

NIKE × SKIMS

Drop culture

Nike's research with Skims silhouettes, aimed at the same core shopper.

4

FABLETICS

Membership

Subscription cash flow that funds new retail stores.

Five angles from Michael Porter's competitor analysis framework.

03 / What I see on the floor

1

COMPLETE LOOKS

Outfits, not pieces

Showing head-to-toe outfits and colour stories could make it easier to buy the whole look.

2

COMFORT FIRST

100% comfort

Guests want comfort even in what they wear to work or out to dinner.

3

SPEED TO TREND

Own the moment

When a silhouette goes viral, like the barrel leg, speed decides who wins it.

4

MEN'S LIFESTYLE

Loyal for years

When men find what works, they stick with it. Room for a sport jacket, overshirt, button-down, knit polo, easy casual pants and a light office layer.

Built from store locations, product drops, executive hires, annual reports and industry press, including lululemon's 2025 Annual Report, Business of Fashion, Modern Retail, CNBC and Earnest Analytics, combined with my own observations from the store floor.
