Projects / Design and craft

Daydreams

I keep a notebook for whatever inspires me. When something catches my eye, I take a picture, print it, stick it in, and doodle around it until I find the patterns hidden inside.

Leek layers

Green vegetables were the starting point here, especially the leek and its layers: how each one wraps around the next, and how the colour moves from white to deep green.

Anime jackets

I've always loved the jackets in anime series, especially Naruto: high collars, hoods, utility vests and layers in muted greens and greys.

Caravaggio's red, and how fabric falls

I tried mixing colours, swatch after swatch, until I found one close to the red in Caravaggio's painting. Next to it, studies of how fabric folds, falls and catches the light.

The Caravaggisti

The paintings of Theodoor Rombouts drew me in because they belong to the Caravaggisti, the painters who followed Caravaggio: that colour palette, that dramatic lighting. I was inspired by their collar patterns, and in the last page, by the whole colour palette.
