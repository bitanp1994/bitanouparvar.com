Visual Merchandising

Mannequins I styled at lululemon, and the thinking behind them.

Styled by a guest question

I overheard a guest asking her friend which colours she could wear with this blue jacket. That question became the outfit.

I paired it with a lighter blue and a warm cream, so anyone with the same question could see the answer on the mannequin.

Yoga and training

These two tell the story of a whole session, not just an outfit. One is turned to show the back of the sports bra, with the yoga mat carried on its strap. The other shows layering for training, with a towel and gloves. Together they bring the accessories into the picture, so guests can see everything they need for class.
