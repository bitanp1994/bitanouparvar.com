Projects / Retail projects

Visual Merchandising

Mannequins I styled at lululemon, and the thinking behind them.

Styled by a guest question

I overheard a guest asking her friend which colours she could wear with this blue jacket. That question became the outfit.

I paired it with a lighter blue and a warm cream, so anyone with the same question could see the answer on the mannequin.

Before and after

The same outfit, styled two ways. On its own, it's a matching set. With a bucket hat, a belt bag and a water bottle, it becomes a sunny day out, and guests can picture where they'd wear it. This was a way to see for myself how much storytelling and styling change the way a look lands.

Yoga and training

These two tell the story of a whole session, not just an outfit. One is turned to show the back of the sports bra, with the yoga mat carried on its strap. The other shows layering for training, with a towel and gloves. Together they bring the accessories into the picture, so guests can see everything they need for class.

Layering and styling

Cropped tops over a bra, with the waistband showing, then a cap and a bag to finish. These looks show how pieces from different parts of the store work together, and give guests an easy way to wear more than one item at once.

Fixture storytelling

A fixture can tell a story too. The yoga one is built around a class: the mat sits on top, then bras, shorts and leggings in an easy colour run, with a jacket for after class and socks and bottles to finish. The casual one is an off-duty wardrobe: everyday basics layered under a statement vest and a utility jacket, with caps, a belt bag and a duffle, so guests can see a full outfit in one place.

Collaboration launch

The lululemon x Disney collaboration needed a gold-standard display. I built each fixture as one complete story, with accessories at eye level and apparel below, so the whole collection reads at a glance from across the floor.
