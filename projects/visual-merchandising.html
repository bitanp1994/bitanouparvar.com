Visual Merchandising

Visual storytelling at lululemon: how styling, colour and product placement turn a mannequin or a fixture into a story guests can see themselves in.

For this mannequin, I was inspired by a guest. I overheard her asking her friend which colours she could wear with this blue jacket.

So I styled it with a lighter blue and a warm cream, to show the answer right on the mannequin for anyone with the same question.

When I first found this mannequin, it looked like the photo on the left. It was a nice set, but it wasn't saying anything. It needed a story. So I added a bucket hat, a belt bag and a water bottle, and it became a sunny day out that guests could picture themselves in.

My intention here was to showcase accessories and equipment. One mannequin is turned to show the back of the sports bra, with the yoga mat carried on its strap. The other has a towel, training gloves and a water bottle, so guests see everything they need for a session, not just the outfit.

With these two, I wanted to play with layering, colour and texture, from gym to street. Cropped tops over a bra with the waistband showing, soft fleece against a technical layer, then a cap and a bag to finish, so one look works for a workout and the rest of the day.

For the lululemon x Disney launch, my intention was a gold-standard presentation. I set up the collaboration mannequins and fixtures to the brand's display standard, so the collection looked polished and ready from day one.
