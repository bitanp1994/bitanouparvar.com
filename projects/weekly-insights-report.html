Weekly Insights Report

A one-page weekly report that turns sales data into something the team can read in five minutes and act on.

The gap

I felt there was a gap in how we made decisions: we didn't have a clear picture of what the numbers were telling us. I suggested closing that gap by translating our data into insights and stories the whole team could benefit from.

The report

Sample report with example numbers.

  • The weekTotal sales against plan and last year.
  • The driversTraffic, conversion, items per guest and average price, each with a one-line explanation.
  • Top sellersThe three best styles in each key category.
  • Next steps"How we can help this week": what's working, what to watch and where the gaps are.
  • FeedbackA QR code to Limu, so the team can add what the numbers don't show.

The check-in

Numbers show what sold, but they can't show why a guest walked out empty-handed. So I built Limu, a short weekly check-in the team fills in on their phones in about two minutes. A QR code on the report leads straight to it.

  • It asks what guests couldn't find, why they left without buying, and what got in the way that week.
  • Before the new questions, it shows what the team said the week before, so everyone can see their answers being used.
  • A lemon mascot keeps it light, so people actually come back to it.

Screens from Limu, with example answers.

The impact

The whole team uses the report to create a better guest experience, and each group puts it to work in its own way:

  • ManagementThe starting point for weekly and monthly business recaps.
  • Back of houseReorganizing the golden zone so the fastest-moving styles are closest to hand.
  • Visual merchandisingDeciding which products to prioritize and how to merchandise them.
