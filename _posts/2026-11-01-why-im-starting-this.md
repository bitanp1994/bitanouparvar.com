I'm going to use this space to share what I'm learning about the fashion industry — especially athleisure, since that's where I work right now.

I come from electrical engineering, so I tend to ask *how does this actually work?* In a store, a lot of the answers are hidden: why we got 40 of one colour and 4 of another, why a style gets marked down after six weeks, why one store sells out and another doesn't. That's the world of merchandising, allocation, and buying — and I want to understand it properly.

Here's roughly what I'll write about:

- **#retailbasics** — short explainers on terms like merchandising, allocation, buying, and sourcing, in plain language.
- **#mathandfashion** — the numbers behind product decisions: sell-through, weeks of cover, the 80/20 rule.
- **#marketnotes** — things I notice on the floor and in the news.
- **#pasttofuture** — where a trend came from and where it might go. (How did leggings become everyday wear?)

No schedule, no pressure. Just notes, written as I learn.
